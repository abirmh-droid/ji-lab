01 / OUR SCIENCE

Small changes.
Significant questions.

From healthy airway cells to disease, we investigate metabolic changes that could help explain how illness begins and progresses.

Research at Penn State ↗
01METABOLISM

Metabolic reprogramming
& early detection

We examine altered nutrient use and redox balance in airway disease. Stable isotope tracing and metabolic flux analysis help reveal changes associated with disease risk.

Stable isotope–resolved metabolomics

02AIRWAY HEALTH

Environmental exposure
& ferroptosis

We investigate how e-cigarette exposure may contribute to chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, including the role of ferroptosis, an iron-dependent form of cell death.

Human stem cell models · COPD

03DISEASE MECHANISMS

Cystic fibrosis
& diabetes

We study how cells with altered CFTR affect pancreatic islets, using stem cell and transgenic models to explore mechanisms of cystic fibrosis–related diabetes.

Cell interactions · Metabolic dysfunction

A BROADER VIEW

Our research also explores probiotics and their potential role in obesity and insulin resistance.
